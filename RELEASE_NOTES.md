Lowspeak now loses fewer dictations and writes your words more reliably.

- **Retry a failed dictation:** when a transcription fails, Lowspeak keeps the recording in memory. **Retry Failed Dictation** in the menu transcribes it again, for example once the network is back or after you chose another model.
- **The text stays where you spoke it:** if another app came to the front before the text was ready, Lowspeak leaves the text on the clipboard and tells you, instead of pasting it into the wrong app.
- **No credit left:** when your OpenAI account has run out of credit, Lowspeak says so instead of asking you to wait, and does not try again in vain.
- **No invented text:** Whisper sometimes wrote text nobody said into silence, such as the credits of subtitled videos. Lowspeak now leaves it out.
- **Your vocabulary with Whisper:** Whisper no longer drops a sentence that begins like the one before it.
- **Your vocabulary with Parakeet:** Parakeet now spells your terms as you wrote them where it came very close, for example Traefik for "Träfig".
